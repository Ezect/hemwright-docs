Hemwright support

Need help with the Hemwright theme? Write to us with the details below and we will look into it.

How to contact support

Send an email to pierpaolofratangelo@gmail.com.

Never send us your password. If we need to look at your store, we will ask for a collaborator request, which you can remove at any time.

Response times

We aim to reply within 2 business days (Monday to Friday, Central European Time). This is a target, not a guarantee: replies can take longer around public holidays or when many requests arrive together. A complete message with the details below helps us answer faster, often in a single reply.

What to include in your request

  1. Your store URL (the myshopify.com address is fine).
  2. The page where the problem happens, with the link.
  3. What you did, what you expected and what happened instead.
  4. Your browser and device (for example Safari on iPhone).
  5. A screenshot or a short screen recording.
  6. If you are not sure the theme is the cause, the list of installed apps.

Before you write

  • Read the documentation, especially the frequently asked questions.
  • Check that Theme settings > Motion effects is set the way you want.
  • If text appears in the wrong language, check the language selected in your store and in your staff account.
  • If you can, try the page in a private window with apps disabled.
  • Before following any guide that edits code, duplicate your theme first.

What support covers

  • Installing and setting up the theme.
  • Using the theme settings, sections and blocks.
  • Bugs and problems in the theme code.
  • Questions about the product page blocks, collection loading, the 3D viewer, motion settings, translations and right-to-left display.

Not covered

  • Custom design or code changes made for your store (for custom work, hire a Shopify Partner).
  • Problems caused by third-party apps or by code you added yourself.
  • Writing your content, photos or product data.
  • Translations for languages the theme does not include.
  • Replying to customers who asked to be told when an item is back in stock: those requests arrive in your store email and you reply yourself.
  • Accessibility of your own content (alt text, colors you choose, videos you add). Accessibility bugs in the theme code are in scope.
